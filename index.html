FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What is a RETURN PLEASE QR sticker? +

It is a small QR sticker designed to help a finder identify the item and follow the available contact process.

Where can I put the sticker? +

You can place it on suitable belongings such as backpacks, laptops, luggage, key holders and other personal items.

Do I need an app? +

The basic concept is designed around scanning a QR code using a phone camera. No dedicated app is required for the basic scan.

Is my phone number printed on the sticker? +

No. The sticker is designed around a QR/ID system rather than publicly printing your personal phone number.
